All raw sequencing datasets analyzed in this study were obtained from public repositories, primarily GEO, under the accession numbers listed in Supplementary Table S1 and in data/accessions/public_dataset_accessions.tsv. Reprocessed metadata, split-design files, frozen IMRS coefficient tables, delivery-minus-control Delta IMRSz summaries, robustness outputs, enrichment outputs, and manuscript-ready supplementary table inputs are available in the repository release candidate at https://github.com/YanshengLuo/InnateImmuneResponseScore. The derived data package includes dataset-level provenance, split-level contrast definitions, manuscript role assignments, late or context-shifted boundary-context annotations, robustness summaries, and retained-gene enrichment results. No new human-subject data were generated for this study.

Raw sequencing files should be retrieved from GEO or other indicated public repositories by accession rather than redistributed in this source repository unless redistribution is separately confirmed as appropriate.
